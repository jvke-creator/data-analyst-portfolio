🌍 European Happiness & Regional Wellbeing

📌 Project Overview

This project explores differences in reported happiness across countries and investigates how wellbeing relates to economic and social indicators.

Business Question

What factors are associated with higher reported wellbeing, and how does wellbeing differ across regions?

---

🎯 Objectives

- Compare happiness scores across countries and regions.
- Examine the relationship between happiness and freedom to make life choices.
- Explore the relationship between wellbeing and economic indicators.
- Investigate social factors associated with happiness.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard to communicate findings.

---

🛠️ Tools Used

- Excel — data preparation and exploration
- SQL — querying and analysis
- Python — data cleaning and exploratory analysis
- Power BI — data modelling, visualisation and dashboard development

---

📊 Key Areas of Analysis

Happiness by Country

Compare countries based on their reported happiness scores.

Regional Comparison

Examine differences in average happiness between regions.

Freedom & Happiness

Investigate whether countries with higher freedom to make life choices also tend to report higher wellbeing.

Economic & Social Factors

Explore how factors such as economic conditions, social support and healthy life expectancy relate to happiness.

---

📈 Power BI Dashboard

The planned dashboard will include:

- Average Happiness Score
- Average Freedom Score
- Average Social Support
- Happiness by Country
- Happiness by Region
- Freedom vs Happiness scatter plot
- Country-level comparison
- Interactive country and region filters
